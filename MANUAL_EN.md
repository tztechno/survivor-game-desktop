# CYBER SURVIVORS - HOW TO PLAY (Game Manual)

『**CYBER SURVIVORS**』 is a fast-paced **top-down auto-attack survivor roguelite ACT** set in a vibrant cyberpunk universe. Mow down endless swarms of cybernetic enemies, harvest energy gems, and craft unbeatable builds to survive against impossible odds.

---

## 1. 🚀 Core Mechanics & Game Flow

All weapons trigger **automatically**. Your primary focus is positioning, dodging enemy entrapments, and harvesting fallen EXP gems.

```
[Maneuver & Kite Swarms] ➔ [Auto-Attack Annihilation] ➔ [Collect EXP Gems] ➔ [Level Up & Enhance Build]
```

### Heads-Up Display (HUD)
- **LV / EXP Bar (Top)**: Fills as you collect EXP gems. Triggers upgrade selection upon filling.
- **⏱️ Survival Timer**: Tracks current run duration. A colossal boss emerges every 60 seconds.
- **💀 Kills / 💎 Score**: Slay count and total points earned.
- **Inventory Bar (Bottom Left)**: Displays currently equipped weapons (max 4) and passive chips (max 5).

## 5. 👾 Enemies & Boss Encounters

- 🔵 **Slime**: Standard swarm enemy that overwhelms through shear numbers.
- 🟣 **Cyber Bat**: High-speed flier using erratic zig-zag flight paths to dive into the player.
- 🟪 **Armored Golem**: Heavyweight cyber juggernaut with elevated HP. Knock back with concentrated fire.
- 👑 **Cyber Boss**: Colossal golden mech appearing every **60 seconds**. Drops massive EXP bounties on defeat!

---

## 6. 💡 Survival & Build Tips

1. **Circular Kiting Technique**:
   - Avoid fleeing in a straight line. Circle around the swarm to compress enemies into a dense cluster and concentrate dropped EXP gems.
2. **Prioritize Maxing 1-2 Weapons Early**:
   - A single Level 4-5 weapon clears swarms exponentially faster than four Level 1 weapons, accelerating your leveling curve.
3. **Magnetic Core Synergies**:
   - Combining long-range weapons with Magnetic Core allows you to safely harvest gems without diving into danger.
4. **Demo Mode (Auto-Pilot)**:
   - Click "DEMO PLAY" on the title screen to watch the autonomous AI in action. Click anywhere or hit any key to take over manual control immediately!
